A complete ISO 27001:2022 & NIST CSF 2.0 aligned ISMS for a fictional export company—demonstrating risk assessment, policy writing, and compliance expertise.
 Overview
This project demonstrates my ability to design and document a lightweight Information Security Management System (ISMS) for a small export company.  
It’s built as a portfolio piece to show practical GRC skills for roles like GRC Analyst, IT Risk Officer.

Fictional Company: Flash Exports (Pty) Ltd  
Industry: Fruit export, KZN → UK/EU  
Date: October 2025

 Objective
Build a practical ISMS aligned to ISO 27001:2022 and NIST CSF 2.0 that addresses compliance requirements for POPIA and UK GDPR.

 Deliverables
All documents are in the `/docs` folder:

1. 01-Information-Security-Policy-v1.0.docx  
   Core policy covering access control, data protection, incident reporting.

2. 02-Risk-Register-Treatment-Plan.xlsx  
   5 realistic risks with likelihood, impact, controls, and treatment plans mapped to ISO 27001 Annex A.

3. 03-Incident-Response-Plan-v1.0.docx  
   1-page IR plan covering detection, containment, notification, and recovery.

4. 04-Security-Awareness-Policy-v1.0.docx  
   Policy for staff training, phishing simulations, and acceptable use.

5. Portfolio-Summary.pdf  
   3-slide summary of the project, problem, solution, and outcome.

 Frameworks & Resources Used
- ISO/IEC 27001:2022 – ISMS structure and Annex A controls
- NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0 – Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover
- POPIA / UK GDPR – Data protection requirements for SA and UK/EU exports
- Professor Messer Security+ – Security fundamentals and awareness content
- Jason Dion Policy Templates – Used as baseline for policy structure

 Skills Demonstrated
- Risk Assessment & Treatment
- Policy and Procedure Writing
- Compliance Mapping: ISO 27001, NIST CSF, POPIA, GDPR
- Incident Response Planning
- Security Awareness Program Design
- Technical Documentation
 How to Use This Repo
1. Review `Portfolio-Summary.pdf` for a 2-minute overview.
2. Open documents in `/docs` to see full detail.
3. All documents are editable. Replace "Apex Exports" with your own fictional company if needed.
